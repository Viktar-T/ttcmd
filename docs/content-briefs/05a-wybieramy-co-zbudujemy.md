## Reader position

Has read 0a–0c, 1b–1f, 2a–2e, 3a–4e. Four repositories; `notatnik-v2` built
from a specification with seven criteria checked with evidence. Has never had a
user who was not themselves (8 of 12), and has no project of their own (12 of

the next lesson asks what to build next.

## Carrying question

Który z twoich pomysłów zbudujesz — i po czym poznasz, że to ten?

## Anchor

**`pomysly.md`** — the file, with three entries of four sentences each (co robi ·
kto go otworzy · po czym poznam · czego nie robi). It enters the lesson as a
collection and leaves it with one entry marked `## WYBRANE` and the other two
kept under a heading that says what they are for.

## Shape

Hands-on, short: opening, two concept sections, the peer round, the choice, the
floor, the ending. No build.

## Owns · recalls · avoids

- **Owns** (appendix rows move from 4f to 5a): „ktoś prawdziwy ma tego używać —
  i ty się liczysz”; the idea as four sentences; **„wybrane, jeszcze nie
  wycenione”** replacing „zebrane, nie wybrane”; the menu of ready-made ideas.
- **Recalls**: 4c's specification, one clause; 4e's one user, one clause;
  `notatnik-v2` as the thing every reader has.
- **Avoids**: any pointer to Moduł 9 (open decision #15); *MVP*, *produkt*,
  *startup*; any invented student, classroom scene or teacher biography; naming
  a stack; the word *wycena* in its Moduł 5b sense — the ending points at the
  next lesson by what it does, not by its term.

## Exercises

1. Recall — the four sentences and the bar, in one sentence, from memory.
2. Action on the anchor — three entries in `pomysly.md` in twenty minutes; the
   second sentence names a person or a kind of person, never „każdy”.
3. Build step — the peer round, then the choice written into the file, then
   commit and push. The file is opened again in the next lesson.
4. Research — ask one person outside the class what small thing they repeat every
   week and would hand to a program; write it as a fourth entry, in their words.

## Decisions

- **The peer round comes before the choice** — rejected: keeping 4f's order
  (round last). Once the lesson chooses, the round has to feed the choice or it
  is decoration.
- **The choice is written into `pomysly.md`, not into a new file** — one file,
  one history, and the rejected ideas stay visible beside the chosen one.
- **The menu is four ideas, described in the four sentences** — rejected: a
  paragraph each. Anything longer reads as a specification and would break the
  module's rule that no lesson names a feature of anyone's application; four
  sentences is the same shape the student's own entries have.
- **The escape hatch has a deadline** — rejected: „możesz zmienić zdanie
  kiedykolwiek”. An idea swapped in the middle of a build week costs the week.
- **`publish: true` kept** — the lesson is live under the old slug; the rewrite
  is a revision of published material, not a new lesson.
