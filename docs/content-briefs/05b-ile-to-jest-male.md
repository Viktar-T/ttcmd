## Reader position

Has read 0a–4e and 5a. Has one chosen idea in `pomysly.md`, four sentences long,
and two more in reserve. Has one week of measured experience: `notatnik-v2` —
one page of specification, seven criteria, six to eight tasks, one commit each.
Has never judged whether an idea fits a given amount of time: the modal largest
program in this class is about a hundred lines, and five of twelve describe
their largest as unfinished and used by nobody.

## Carrying question

Skąd wiesz, że to, co wybrałeś, zmieści się w czasie, który masz — zanim
zaczniesz to budować?

## Anchor

**The chosen entry from `pomysly.md`, laid beside `notatnik-v2`.** The notatnik
is the measuring rod, because it is the one program every reader has built to a
specification and can count: pages of specification, criteria, tasks, screens.
The lesson ends with the entry rewritten — same first two sentences, a longer
„czego nie robi”, and a `pozniej.md` holding what was cut.

## Shape

Narrative for two sections, then hands-on: the cut is made in the lesson, on the
student's own entry.

## Owns · recalls · avoids

- **Owns** (proposed appendix rows): *apetyt* against *wycena* — Singer's two
  sentences and „fixed time, variable scope”; the three ways an idea is too big
  and their three cuts; `pozniej.md`; „pierwsza wersja na jeden ekran”.
- **Recalls**: 4c's size rule („jeśli zmianę da się opisać jednym zdaniem, nie
  pisze się do niej specyfikacji”), one clause, by link; 5a's four sentences.
- **Avoids**: story points, velocity, man-hours, any estimation technique the
  course does not use; naming a framework; anything that presumes what the
  application does.

## Exercises

1. Recall — the three ways an idea is too big and the cut for each.
2. Action on the anchor — count your idea against the notatnik: how many
   sentences „co robi” needs, how many criteria you would have to check, how many
   screens. Write the three numbers down.
3. Build step — the cut entry and `pozniej.md`, committed and pushed.
4. Reflection / peer — give your cut entry to a classmate with one question: „czy
   zbudowałbyś to w dwa tygodnie?” Write down the answer and, if it is no, the
   one thing they would cut next.

## Reader assumptions to verify

- That „two weeks of building” is what 5e and 5f actually get. The draft says
  „dwa tygodnie budowania” and never a date, a lesson count or an hour count.
- That it is acceptable to state the survey's modal-largest-program figure in a
  student-facing lesson. It is aggregate and carries no name (Article IV), but
  it is the class reading about itself, which is Viktar's call.

## Decisions

- **Singer is quoted, not paraphrased** — the two sentences are the mechanism,
  and they are short enough to be recognised again later; the quote carries
  author, work, link and the date it was checked, per the sources rule.
- **The rod is `notatnik-v2`, not a number of lines** — rejected: „mniej niż
  N linijek”. Lines are not a size a student can judge before building, and a
  line count would be a claim the course cannot source.
- **`pozniej.md` is a separate file, not a section of the specification** —
  rejected: „czego nie robi” carrying everything. A cut feature that lives in
  the specification keeps arguing for itself.
- **No estimation technique is taught** — the lesson teaches the opposite move,
  and says so.
