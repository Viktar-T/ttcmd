But AI is not only about text. Some models can see — or rather, generate images — and that is where **diffusion models** come in.

## Diffusion models

A diffusion model starts from random noise and gradually improves it, step by step, until it becomes a sharp image. This technique is the basis of tools like Midjourney, DALL·E, Stable Diffusion, and FLUX. It turns abstract prompts into beautiful visual images — and does so with striking precision.

## Diffusion models in programming

Today there are several examples of using diffusion models to generate code:

- **Gemini Diffusion** from Google
- **DiffuCoder** from Apple
- **RND1 30B** from Radical Numerics

They are also called **Diffusion Language Models (DLM)**, and they show a significant speed-up in generation compared with classical LLMs. They have not yet become widespread, though, and they are not the main architecture for code generation.
