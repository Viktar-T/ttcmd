# Foundation models

**Source (RU):** Фундаментальные модели  
**Path:** Home → Basic Theory → Level “Beginner” → Foundation models  
**Published:** ~10 months ago

Everything we have seen so far — understanding text and images, reasoning, multimodality — are traits of powerful base models called **foundation models**.

A foundation model is a large AI model trained on huge, diverse datasets. It learns general knowledge and patterns across domains — not just for one task. After training, it can be **fine-tuned** or adapted for many specific uses: conversation, programming, writing, summarization, translation, and more.

Instead of building new models from scratch, developers use foundation models as a base. That makes AI development faster, cheaper, and more scalable.
