At this point you may be wondering — with so many models, which ones are the best?

This is where the term **SOTA** comes in. It is short for **State of the Art** — a popular nickname for the strongest models available at a given moment.

When people say a model is “SOTA”, they mean it is among the best on public **benchmarks**: tasks such as reading comprehension, math, reasoning, programming, and others.

“SOTA” is not an official name. It is a community label — based on performance, reputation, and real-world use. A model can be called SOTA one month and then be replaced by something better the next.

## Where to see who is SOTA right now

The leaderboard changes every few weeks, so it helps to know where it is tracked:

- [**LMArena**](https://lmarena.ai/) — a model ranking based on blind pairwise comparisons: users vote for the better of two anonymous answers.
- [**Artificial Analysis**](https://artificialanalysis.ai/) — independent measurements of model intelligence, speed, and cost, combined into unified indexes.
- [**SWE-bench**](https://www.swebench.com/) — the main benchmark for evaluating models on real programming tasks (fixing bugs in actual open-source repositories).
