## Tools, libraries, and real workflows

You now understand how LLMs work under the hood — from architecture through training and optimization. This level is about putting that knowledge into practice: which tools to use, how to structure workflows, and what makes AI work in real conditions.

We will also look at advanced strategies such as **meta-prompting**, **tool use**, and the hardware that makes all of this possible.
