# Artificial Superintelligence

**Source (RU):** Artificial Superintelligence  
**Path:** Home → Basic Theory → Level “Horizons” → Artificial Superintelligence  
**Published:** ~10 months ago

**Artificial Superintelligence** (ASI, superintelligence) is a hypothetical kind of AI that far exceeds any human in every possible respect. It is the next step after AGI.

While AGI should match human intelligence, ASI would go much further. It could solve problems faster, think more deeply, and improve again and again without human help.

Key ideas of ASI:

- **Self-improvement** — ASI could rewrite its own code, create better versions of itself, and learn at an extraordinary speed
- **Creative thinking** — it could make new scientific discoveries, develop advanced technologies, or understand things that are closed to us
- **Global impact** — if ASI is built, it could change everything: science, the economy, politics, and even our place in the universe

ASI is not just a more powerful tool — it would be a new form of intelligence beyond human understanding and control.

For now ASI remains theory. Nobody knows whether it will be built, or when. But many researchers and companies agree that if it happens, we need to prepare carefully — because it could become either the greatest invention in history, or the most dangerous.
