## What it is

The **technological singularity** (singularity) is a hypothetical moment in the future when technology starts advancing so fast that people can no longer understand or control it.

Many researchers and futurists think the singularity might come after AGI or ASI is built — especially if those systems start improving themselves at high speed.

One of the best-known thinkers behind this idea is **Ray Kurzweil** — scientist, Google executive, futurist, and inventor, known for bold forecasts.

*Portrait on the platform: Raymond Kurzweil — paste it here if you want it in the local notes.*

In his book [*The Singularity Is Near*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Singularity_Is_Near) he suggests:

- AGI may appear by **2029**
- the singularity may happen around **2045**

Kurzweil believes exponential growth in compute and AI will lead to a point where machines surpass humans in intelligence and start transforming the world at an unstoppable speed.

## Key ideas

- **Uncontrolled growth** — AI systems would be able to learn, evolve, and create new technologies faster than any human team
- **Unpredictable change** — once AI is smarter than people, it may solve problems in ways we do not expect — or cannot track
- **Beyond human understanding** — human intelligence may not be enough to follow or steer future progress

In this scenario, humanity’s future may be shaped by machines smarter than we are — for better or for worse.

The singularity idea remains largely speculative. Some, like Kurzweil, think it could happen in the next two decades; others are more skeptical. Most agree: if it happens, it will change everything.
