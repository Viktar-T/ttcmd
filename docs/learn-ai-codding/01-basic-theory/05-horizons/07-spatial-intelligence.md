## What it is about

When we picture the future of AI — from [AGI](01-agi.md) and [superintelligence](02-asi.md) to rapid technological change — we often think about **how smart** AI can become.

But there is another important question:  
Can AI also **see, move, and create in the real world**, the way people do?

That leads to another kind of intelligence that is deeply human — but very different from language or logic: **spatial intelligence**.

**Spatial intelligence** is the human ability to understand and mentally manipulate objects in three-dimensional space. It lets us:

- imagine how things look from different angles
- navigate complex environments
- visualize, design, and build — from sandcastles to skyscrapers

It is what helps architects plan buildings, pilots fly planes, gamers master virtual worlds, and children stack LEGO towers. Spatial intelligence is tightly tied to how we move through the physical world and shape it.

## Why it matters for AI

So far AI has excelled at language and 2D visualization — generating text, images, and video. The next frontier is the **three-dimensional world**.

*Portrait on the platform: Fei-Fei Li — paste it here if you want it in the local notes.*

**World Labs**, founded by **Fei-Fei Li** and other visionaries in AI and computer vision, is building **Large World Models (LWM)** — foundation models that understand 3D environments and interact with them. Their mission is to take AI beyond flat pixels into spatially intelligent systems that can:

- perceive and reason about objects, spaces, and motion in real dimensions
- generate and edit full 3D worlds with physics, logic, and interactivity
- support creators and professionals — artists, designers, developers, engineers — in imagining and building without limits

## What it unlocks

With spatially intelligent AI we can:

- create immersive virtual worlds with little effort
- build tools for architecture, robotics, AR/VR, and simulation
- let AI understand not only **what** something is — but **where** it is and how it moves or relates to other things

## In short

Spatial intelligence is how people understand space. World Labs is teaching AI to do the same — so it can help us build better, richer, more interactive worlds.
