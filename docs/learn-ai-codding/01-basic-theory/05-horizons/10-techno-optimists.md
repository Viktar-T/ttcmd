# Techno-optimists

**Source (RU):** Техно-оптимисты  
**Path:** Home → Basic Theory → Level “Horizons” → Techno-optimists  
**Published:** ~10 months ago

As AI gets stronger, it also becomes more **political** — not in the sense of governments, but in how people think about its future.

One group of thinkers, engineers, and founders is known as **effective accelerationists**, or simply **techno-optimists**.

They believe rapid growth of artificial intelligence will bring huge benefit to humanity — not only in productivity, but in solving global problems and expanding the limits of human potential.

From their point of view:

- Smarter AI will help create a **post-scarcity society**, where goods and services are available and cheap.
- [AGI](01-agi.md) and [ASI](02-asi.md) could lead to scientific breakthroughs, longevity, or even space exploration.
- The faster we move forward, the faster we unlock those benefits.

This movement is often called **e/acc** (effective accelerationism). It is common among engineers, tech founders, and researchers who see speed, scale, and ambition as necessary for progress.

A well-known figure tied to this thinking is [Ray Kurzweil](03-technological-singularity.md), who already appeared in this level: he forecasts a technological singularity and believes superintelligent systems can greatly expand human capabilities.
