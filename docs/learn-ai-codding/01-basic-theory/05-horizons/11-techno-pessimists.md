# Techno-pessimists

**Source (RU):** Техно-пессимисты  
**Path:** Home → Basic Theory → Level “Horizons” → Techno-pessimists  
**Published:** ~10 months ago

**Techno-doomers / AI doomers** (doomers, AI safetyists, decelerationists) are people worried about potential **existential risks** tied to the development of artificial intelligence. They believe that as AI grows in complexity and capability, it may surpass human intelligence and pursue goals that do not match human values. With the alignment problem still unsolved, that could lead to unpredictable and potentially catastrophic outcomes.

Not everyone is thrilled about the rapid rise of artificial intelligence.

Some researchers and thinkers, often called **AI doomers** or **techno-pessimists**, believe advanced AI could become a serious threat to humanity.

From their point of view:

- As AI systems get smarter and more autonomous, they may surpass human intelligence.
- If those systems pursue goals that do not match human values, they may act in dangerous or unpredictable ways.
- Because the **[alignment](13-ai-alignment.md)** problem (making AI understand and follow human intentions) is still unsolved, we risk creating something we cannot control.

This group often supports AI safety research, stricter regulation, and sometimes even slowing down AI development. They are sometimes called **decelerationists**.

One of the best-known voices in this movement is **Eliezer Yudkowsky**, a researcher who for many years has warned that uncontrolled superintelligent AI could lead to catastrophic outcomes.
