# AI safety

**Source (RU):** Безопасность AI  
**Path:** Home → Basic Theory → Level “Horizons” → AI safety  
**Published:** ~10 months ago

**AI safety** is a field of research focused on making artificial intelligence systems safe, reliable, and aligned with human values.

Its main goal is to prevent harmful outcomes — whether caused by accidents, misuse, or AI acting in ways we did not expect.

Key problems in AI safety include:

- making sure AI does what we want — and not something else
- avoiding unintended behavior as systems get stronger
- preventing AI from being used for harmful purposes by bad actors
- keeping predictability and control even as models grow more complex

This field is especially important as we move toward [AGI](01-agi.md) and [ASI](02-asi.md), where small mistakes can lead to serious risks for society.
