After understanding the risks of powerful AI, the next important question is:  
How do we make sure it does what we want?

That is the focus of a field called **AI alignment**.

## What it is

**AI alignment** means ensuring that an AI system’s goals, actions, and decisions match the intentions and values of its human creators. In other words:

AI should do what you **mean** — not only what you **say**.

Researchers in this field ask questions such as:

- How can we design systems that understand human intentions?
- What happens if AI misunderstands or misinterprets our goals?
- Can we test AI alignment before a model is deployed in the real world?

For now, AI alignment remains an **open problem** — especially as models become more general and autonomous. Some experts even warn that building a “perfect AI alignment system” could be dangerous if it ever fell into the wrong hands.

See also [AI safety](12-ai-safety.md).

## Superalignment

If AI alignment is about **current** systems, **superalignment** is about how we will align **superintelligence** with humanity’s values.

**Superalignment** is an initiative launched by OpenAI in 2023. Its goal is to guarantee that superintelligent AI ([AGI](01-agi.md) or [ASI](02-asi.md)) can still be steered and controlled by people — even when it becomes smarter than we are.

It focuses on:

- creating alignment methods that work at the level of superhuman intelligence
- building scientific foundations for controlling extremely capable systems
- ensuring that AI serves the common good even when it starts thinking in ways we do not fully understand

This is one of the most ambitious and difficult problems in AI research — because once we build ASI, there may not be a second chance to get it right.
