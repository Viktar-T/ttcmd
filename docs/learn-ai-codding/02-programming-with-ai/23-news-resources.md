There are thousands of sites, newsletters, Telegram channels, and other places that write about AI news. To stay in the current news agenda, though, it is enough to follow literally a few sources listed below (with an emphasis on Russian):

- The most concentrated information about AI in development is in the club **AI digests**. These are 10–30 minute mini-podcasts with text transcripts that cover all the main AI news for developers and come out twice a month. In general, the digests are enough to stay on top of the main events 😎

- If you like podcasts, subscribe to the podcast **[На Вайбе](https://podcast.onvibe.io)** (“On Vibe”) — the largest Russian-language podcast on AI news with a tilt toward development. It comes out once every two weeks on all audio platforms and on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@podcastonvibe). Also: [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/show/1XECQwNsnkCD9zxJxPWHFc), [Podbean](https://onvibe.podbean.com/).

- From Telegram channels, periodically scroll through [**Data Secrets**](https://t.me/data_secrets) and [**Machinelearning**](https://t.me/ai_machinelearning_big_data). Their names may look intimidating, but in practice these channels aggregate AI news in development.

- No decent Russian-language email newsletters on AI have been noticed yet, so look at these English-language ones: [**The Rundown AI**](https://www.therundown.ai/) and [**AlphaSignal**](https://alphasignal.ai/). The first is mass-market and covers a wide range of AI topics; the second is more niche and aimed at developers.

- A good English-language podcast and YouTube channel: [**Machine Learning Street Talk**](https://www.youtube.com/@MachineLearningStreetTalk)

💡 These resources are enough to stay in developer news shape. And if you want more, look into the club channel **Info resources**.

The club itself: [evocoders.ai](https://evocoders.ai) / [learn.evocoders.ai](https://learn.evocoders.ai/).
